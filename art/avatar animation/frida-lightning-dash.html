Port this

How it's built: three nested layers, each owning one axis — .dash-track travels horizontally (percentages resolve against the stage width, so it stays responsive), .dash-hop owns the vertical arc with per-keyframe easing, .dash-fig owns squash and lean. Separate layers are what let you retime the hops without touching the traverse.

Porting: mount as an overlay inside .app with pointer-events: none, unmount after the badge settles. The streak counter belongs in Flitsen.grade() — reset on a wrong answer, fire at 3. Frida is the canonical frida-happy.svg, inlined and never redrawn; only transforms animate, so it stays GPU-composited.

iOS: no box-shadow on the moving figure and no 3D transforms — the Safari rasterisation traps you already hit on the card flip. prefers-reduced-motion is honoured: the badge appears, nothing moves.
